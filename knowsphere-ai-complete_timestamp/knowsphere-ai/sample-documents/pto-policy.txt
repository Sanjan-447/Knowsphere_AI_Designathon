§1 Accrual Rates
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off per month, totaling 21 days per year. Accrual begins on the first day of employment and unused days carry over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following year.

§2 Requesting Time Off
Time off requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal. Requests are approved by your direct manager based on team coverage.

§3 Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
